# Preparing My PhD Journey: The Expandsion and Specification of My Research Interest

## Exploring and Developing Internal Representation in Neural Networks Can Be the Game Changer

Based on the questions that I asked to Professor Dilek, I became more certain about the necessity of studying internal representation of neural network, that is one of my specific research interest for interpretable and explainable AI as well. Because how to approach the interpertation of the internal representation of neural networks can be diverse as Professor Dilek explained as well, so I understand that there are researchers who are skeptical about this subject, reflecting the problem formulation process of my interpretable, explainable AI studies, supervised under Professor Jae Sung Lee, Professor Jiook Cha, and Professor Hyungjin Chung as well. However, at the same time, as Professor Dilek answered, exploring the internal representation of neural networks have the potential to be the game changer, and I think it is because the exploration, findings, and optimization of the internal representation of neural network is one of the foundational layer of LLM, LMM, AI agent, the multi-agent collaborations, which have broad impact their efficiency, safety. So in this case, therefore, I understood focusing more on thoughtful designing, building, and examining about how to approach internal representation of neural networks can be one of the important research questions as well.

# My Learning About What Makes AI Research More Valuable: Preparing PhD Application

i learned that valuable research contribution does not always require inventing an entirely new task as far as i observed. Revealing an overlooked aspect of an existing problem, providing a formulation that enables systematic analysis may itself constitute a meaningful contribution as well. From this perspective, i summarized 5 complementary dimensions of research contribution that i have learned (this would be improved as i learn more): <br>

Problem formulation value: Research can contribute by identifying, formulating, or reframing problems that are important yet insufficiently explored. The value of a problem formulation may arise from a combination of its long-term sustainability as a research problem, practical significance (such as urgency, range and degree of its impact), and the degree of under-exploration (finding new under-explored tasks may require deeper domain knowledge). <br>

Methodological value: Research may contribute through novel and sound approaches to solving or understanding a problem. Such contributions can arise at multiple levels, including algorithms, network architectures, pipelines, loss functions, evaluation metrics, dataset construction, learning paradigms, and conceptual or theoretical frameworks toward existing tasks or problems. The efficiency of implementing the proposed method may also be an important consideration. <br>

Experimental value: Experiments can constitute a substantive research contribution when they provide strong and reliable empirical evidence beyond demonstrating isolated performance gains. This value may include improvements in performance, robustness, generalizability across datasets, domains (the domain can be represented as datasets as well), tasks, and experimental settings, as well as systematic identification of failure cases and fine-grained analyses of the proposed methods. Such evidence can clarify not only whether an approach works, but also when, where, and why it succeeds or fails. <br>

Value of findings: AI research can also generate new knowledge beyond performance improvement on established tasks. Such findings may reveal previously unknown properties, limitations, or produce new scientific, medical, mathematical, or domain-specific insights or knowledge.<br>

Ethical value: Research can contribute to humanity, and safety considerations are emphasized.

These dimensions are complementary rather than mutually exclusive as far as i learned. A single study may contribute along several dimensions—for example, by formulating an under-explored problem, proposing a new methodology, demonstrating its generalizability, and revealing previously unknown properties of AI systems. Preparing PhD application and beyond, i hope i can deepen my understanding of research values to contribute to a better society. <br>

These reflections are based on what i have learned under the supervision of the professors, reviewer feedback, and the papers i have read. But as always, my opinions represent my personal reflection of what i have learned and is not the representation of the vision of the professors or mentors who have supervised or supervising me. My willingness to share what i have learned is also shaped by the environments i grew up in.
